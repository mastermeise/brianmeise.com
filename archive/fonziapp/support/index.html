Frequently asked questions

When I start a party, songs start playing. Why?

Fonzi starts the party as soon as possible by playing music at all times. When you start a party, Fonzi picks random songs from your iTunes library. Whenever there are song requests, Fonzi will play those instead. If there are no songs in the queue, it will again pick random songs from your iTunes library.

Do you copy my songs?

Absolutely not. Songs play from your device on your device at all times.

What songs can be requested by my guests?

Any song which is in your iTunes library can be requested for playback.

I've queued up my songs, when will they play?

Fonzi is fair. It round-robins song requests as they arrive. If there's only one person uploading song requests for playback, all his/her songs will play in order. If there are other guests who have uploaded songs too, song requests are alternated between guests for playback. Your song will play - it's only a matter of when! Think of it like your song requests at your local karaoke bar. It might be a packed bar full of singers, but you'll get your time to shine - assuming the karaoke DJ is as fair as Fonzi is.

What happens if there are no songs in the song queue?

Fonzi will pick and play random songs from your iTunes library.

What information do you post to Facebook?

If you choose to post to Facebook when a party starts, we share information to your wall about the party - the party name and description. It's totally up to you though - tap the box off if you don't want anything posted to Facebook.

What usage data do you collect?

We're interested in which functionality is used (or not), and how many people are using it. It helps us shape product decisions for the future. All data is anonymous and aggregated. Again, It's up to you - you can turn it off in the settings section in the app.

Is Fonzi only for use at parties?

Hell no. Use it as a music jukebox anywhere, anytime. A party, your favorite café, your office, the gym. Fonzi is about listening to your music with or without friends. In fact, if you come up with an imaginative idea on how to use Fonzi, let us know!

I still have questions or some feedback - how do I contact you?

Email us at team@fonziapp.com. We love feedback in all forms - good or bad.
